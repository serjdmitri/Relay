Voice, Mac, hologram, hand and phone features, and photo-made models that pull apart along their own creases.

## What's new
- **Voice:** keeps listening after an answer, stops when you talk over it, a calm British "Jarvis" voice, different voices for different jobs, and "Hey Jarvis" limited to chosen apps (optional).
- **Mac:** quick maths and unit conversions, window layouts you can save and recall, focus mode by voice, copy text from the screen, a nightly Downloads tidy-up (shows its plan first), zip and convert with undo.
- **Holograms:** a library with pictures, saved scenes, notes pinned to spots, real (life) size, a print check, stickies, and photo-made models that pull apart along their creases and colours.
- **Hands:** draw shapes in the air with a fist and a pinch; pause music when you leave the desk (opt-in).
- **Phone:** quick-action buttons, messages when a long job finishes (opt-in), lock-screen push-to-talk notes.
- **Activity log:** an Undo button on actions that can be put back.

Tested with 486 unit tests and a check of the installed app. Most of the new features have not been used on real hardware (camera, phone, lock screen, a real printer).

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
