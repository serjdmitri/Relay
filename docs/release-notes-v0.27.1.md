Fixes "Make a Shortcut key" showing nothing, and makes the iPhone Shortcut for you.

## What changed
- The Shortcut key now stays on screen (it was wiped by the page redrawing).
- **Save the Shortcut file** makes `Relay.shortcut` in Downloads, ready to AirDrop to your iPhone.

Not tested: importing the file on a real iPhone.

**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
