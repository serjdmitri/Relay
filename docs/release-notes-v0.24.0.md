More hand gestures, and bigger controls while your hands are in use.

## What changed
- **Controls double in size** while a hand is in view, then go back.
- **Two fists pulled apart** take the model apart; pushed together puts it back. **Two pinches** resize; turned like a wheel they roll the model.
- **Swipe** for the next or previous model; **thumb up** keeps a hologram, **thumb down** closes it.

Tested with scripted hand sequences and real hologram windows. Not tested by me: a live camera and hand.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
