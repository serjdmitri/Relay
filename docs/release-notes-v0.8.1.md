Voice OS-style circling and a black, centered Recent menu.

## What changed
- **Circling:** a smoke of soft blue puffs follows the pointer and thins out behind it, instead of a drawn line.
- **Recent menu:** black like the notch, centered on the left edge like a Dock, popping out and tucking back like liquid glass.

Not tested by me: the Recent menu on more than one display or over full-screen apps.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
