Background agents, with progress in the top right.

## What changed
- **"In the background, research …"** or **"start an agent to …"**: Relay answers at once, and the agent works by itself. Progress (current step, time, then a short result) shows in the top right.
- **Up to three at once.** "What are my agents doing?", "stop all agents", or Stop Background Agents in the menu bar.
- **They never take your mouse.** Agents cannot see or control the screen and cannot send or delete anything. They can search the web, read pages and files you dropped, read and write Notes and Reminders, and read the Calendar.

Not tested by me: a full agent job in the app with a real research task (the manager, commands and the screen panel have tests, and two real Claude sessions ran side by side on separate tool servers).

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
