Cut that works on every hologram, drag a hologram out as a file, measure, drop files on the reactor, and Send to Relay from the iPhone share sheet.

## What changed
- **Cut** now slides along the screen's directions, so it always shows something; the cut sheet is drawn.
- **The hologram is a file:** the **Drag to an app** button hands it to Fusion, a slicer, a folder or a message (STL, 3MF or STEP, a setting).
- **Measure** two points in millimetres.
- **Drop files on the reactor.**
- **Send to Relay** in the iPhone share sheet (a Shortcut Relay makes for you).

Not tested: the real drag into another app, a file dropped on the reactor, a trackpad pinch, and the share-sheet Shortcut on an iPhone.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
