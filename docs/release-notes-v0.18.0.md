3D models are now holograms you can move, pin and combine.

## What changed
- **A hologram, not a window.** No menu, no border, no background: the model is drawn as light, standing on a glowing base, right on your screen.
- **Drag the base to move it anywhere.** Drag the model to turn it, scroll to resize. Clicks outside the model go to your apps.
- **Pin it** to keep it on screen without the Jarvis reactor, even after a new request or a restart.
- **Other models:** arrows step through the real models Relay found (and the built-from-shapes one).
- **Combine:** drop one hologram on another, or say "add a heart to it".

Checked in real Electron windows: how they look, that clicks fall through off the model and are taken on it, stepping options, combining, and pinning (only links are saved). Not tested by me: dragging a base with a real mouse, and asking for a hologram by voice in the app.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
