Recent asks on the left edge, a comet trail when you circle, and captions in the top right.

## What changed
- **Recent asks:** rest the pointer on the left edge and a glass list of your latest asks slides out. Click one to ask it again. Off switch: General, or the menu bar icon.
- **Circling:** the stroke is a comet that fades away behind the pointer while you draw.
- **Heard nothing?** The reactor fades away in the middle instead of flying to the corner.
- **The corner reactor** glows as much as the big one.
- **Captions** in the top right while Relay works: your request, the current step, then the answer.

Not tested by me: the Recent panel on more than one display or over full-screen apps.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
