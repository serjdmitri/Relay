Cleaner hologram buttons, easier hand control, STEP/3MF export, and holograms on your phone.

## What changed
- **Hologram buttons off the model.** Settings: Menu (one ⋯ button), Side, or Bar. They never sit on top of the model.
- **Wait after you touch it.** A hologram stays still for 3 s / 10 s / 30 s / 2 min after you move it, then turns again. A setting.
- **Hand control:** a switch in the menu bar and in Settings, with a gesture guide. **Point** at a piece and hold the **peace sign** to ask about it by voice. **Pinch-drag** in empty space to draw a glowing line.
- **Save as STL, 3MF or STEP,** or send it to Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, Cura, Creality Print, Fusion, FreeCAD or Blender.
- **Phone view** of your holograms in Relay Remote: turn, zoom, save.
- **Drop a file** on a hologram: STL/GLB become a model, other files go with your next question.
- **GitHub page** updated and colour matched to the app.

Tested with unit tests, real hologram windows and a real browser for the phone page. Not tested by me: a live camera and hand, a real Finder drag onto a hologram, the phone against a real Mac over the network.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
