Say "hands" to see what the camera sees.

## What changed
- **"Hands"** turns hand control on and opens the **hand view** in the top right: the camera, the outline of each hand, what each hand is doing, how many hands there are, and what Relay did with it. "Hide hands" puts it away; "stop hands" turns control off. Also in Settings (Show hand view) and the menu bar.
- The view never takes a click, stays out of Relay's screenshots, and its picture is never saved or sent.

Tested with the real camera (1 hand, 30 fps) and the spoken commands in the installed app. Not tested: two hands at once, every gesture on the live camera.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
