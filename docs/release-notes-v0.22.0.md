The reactor stays with your holograms. Design in 3D by voice and edit by hand. Hand control. A safe Mac drive. Composio.

## What changed
- **Reactor stays while holograms are up** until you close it or pin what to keep; Esc leaves the holograms. The flicker of the finished task is gone.
- **Design with shapes by voice, edit like Tinkercad** (resize, move, holes, add, remove, undo), **save as STL** and open in a slicer or Fusion. Pick where a combined piece connects by dragging it onto a surface. Click a part and ask about it.
- **Mac drive:** find files with previews; tidy folders from a plan you see first; undo. **Relay never deletes:** destructive commands are refused in code, the Trash is move-only and never emptied.
- **Composio / any MCP server** in Settings > Connectors (reads run, changes ask, deletes refused).
- **Hand control (camera, opt-in):** fist grabs, pinch clicks, two hands resize, peace sign opens the mic. Runs on this Mac, no pictures kept or sent.

Tested in real Electron windows and on real folders. Not tested by me: hand control with a live camera, Composio with a real account, and the Trash move.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
