A brighter reactor with a glow, and no panel behind it in the corner.

## What changed
- The reactor is brighter and glows, with a dark shadow so it reads on a white window too.
- In the top left corner there is no background panel any more, only a shadow behind the reactor and its text. The dark blue veil behind the big reactor in the middle stays.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.

## Install
Download the `.dmg` for your Mac and drag Relay to Applications. It is not notarized, so the first time, right-click Relay and choose Open, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Relay.app`.
