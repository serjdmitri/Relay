3D models you can turn.

## What changed
- **"Show me a 3D model of a skull."** A black glass viewer pops out; drag to turn, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. Esc or × closes it.
- **Real models first, from Wikimedia Commons,** with the maker and license shown. Skull, heart, brain, Eiffel Tower and chess knight all turned up and loaded in under a second. Coverage is patchy, and some models lie on their side: Turn upright cycles the up-direction.
- **No real model? Relay builds a simple one from shapes** (an atom, the solar system, a molecule) and says it is a stand-in.
- Off switch in General > 3D models.

Tested in real Electron: a real downloaded model, the shapes model, and the Turn upright button (screenshots of what the windows drew). Not tested by me: asking for a 3D model by voice in the app.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
