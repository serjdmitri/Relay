Hold to talk on the phone is tried in Safari, and says why when it is blocked.

## What changed
- The phone page now tries hold to talk in any browser with speech recognition (Safari), even on plain home Wi-Fi. If the microphone is blocked on that address, it says so and points to the Tailscale https link. Browsers without speech recognition are told to open Relay in Safari.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
