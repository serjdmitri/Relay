Pinch to zoom, a cut bar to see inside holograms, and editing holograms from your phone.

## What changed
- **Pinch the trackpad** over a hologram to zoom it.
- **Cut through it:** a bar you slide across the model to take material away and see inside (X, Y or Z, flip the side). Also on the phone.
- **Edit from your phone:** change the shapes of a model you designed, or tell Relay what to change about any hologram.

Tested with real hologram windows and a phone-sized browser. Not tested: a real trackpad, the phone against a real Mac, and "tell Relay" with the real model.

## Requirements
**macOS 26 or later.** Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) builds are attached below.
